The funniest one-line farewell a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent.

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`: the phone itself speaking,
in its own voice, about its own dying battery — a keeper of secrets that has
held onto everything its owner would rather forget, and is taking the good
gossip with it as the last percent drains away. No will, no charger goodbye,
no pun on 1 percent: just a petty little phone with one percent left and a
final beat at the end of the line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The entry text is written to `/output/entry.txt` (set `ENTRY_OUTPUT` to write
elsewhere when checking locally).
